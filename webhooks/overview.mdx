Webhooks notify your server when an event happens — a payment succeeds, a bank account is added, a user's KYC is approved — so you don't have to poll the API.

## How It Works

1. You register an HTTPS endpoint and choose the events it receives.
2. When an event occurs, Finogates sends it to your endpoint as a JSON `POST`.
3. Your endpoint verifies the signature and responds with a `2xx` status.
4. Failed deliveries are retried.

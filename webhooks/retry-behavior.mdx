---
title: "Retry Behavior"
description: "How Finogates retries webhook deliveries."
icon: "rotate"
---

A delivery succeeds when your endpoint responds with a `2xx` status within **10 seconds**. Any other status, a timeout, or a connection error is retried. Redirects are not followed.

## Schedule

Each event is attempted up to **7 times**:

| Attempt | Sent after the previous attempt |
|---------|---------------------------------|
| 1       | Immediately                     |
| 2       | ~3 seconds                      |
| 3       | ~1 minute                       |
| 4       | ~12 minutes                     |
| 5       | ~1 hour                         |
| 6       | ~4 hours                        |
| 7       | ~13 hours                       |

Delays include a small random jitter. After the 7th failed attempt, the delivery is marked failed and is not retried.

## Recommendations

- Respond `2xx` as soon as the event is received, and process it asynchronously.
- De-duplicate by the event `id` — it is the same on every attempt.
- Don't rely on delivery order; use `created_at`.

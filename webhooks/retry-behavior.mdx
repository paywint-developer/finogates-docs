---
title: "Retry Behavior"
description: "How we retry webhook deliveries."
icon: "rotate"
---

A delivery succeeds when your endpoint responds with a `2xx` status within **10 seconds**. We retry on any other status, a timeout or a connection error. We do not follow redirects.

## Schedule

We try each event up to **7 times**:

| Attempt | Sent after the previous attempt |
|---------|---------------------------------|
| 1       | Immediately                     |
| 2       | ~3 seconds                      |
| 3       | ~1 minute                       |
| 4       | ~12 minutes                     |
| 5       | ~1 hour                         |
| 6       | ~4 hours                        |
| 7       | ~13 hours                       |

Each delay also changes by a small random amount. After the 7th failed attempt, we mark the delivery as failed and stop retrying.

## Recommendations

- Respond with `2xx` as soon as you receive the event. Process it after you respond.
- Spot duplicates with the event `id`. It is the same on every attempt.
- Don't rely on delivery order. Use `created_at`.

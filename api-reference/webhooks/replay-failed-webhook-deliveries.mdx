---
title: 'Replay Failed Webhook Deliveries'
description: "Try sending your failed webhook notifications again."
openapi: 'POST /v1/platform/webhooks/deliveries/replay-failed'
---

Sometimes a notification can't reach your server, for example when your server was briefly down. This call takes those failed notifications and tries to send them again right away.

## Choosing what to resend

You can add one or more of these optional filters:

- `delivery_id`: resend just one specific notification.
- `event_id`: resend every failed notification for one specific event.
- `endpoint_id`: resend every failed notification for one specific webhook.
- `limit`: the most notifications to resend at once. The default is 100, and the most you can set is 500.

If you add no filters, we resend the most recent failed notifications, up to the `limit`.

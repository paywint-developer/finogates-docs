---
title: 'Replace Beneficial Owners'
description: "Send the whole set of business owners to replace the previous set."
openapi: 'PUT /v1/platform/users/{user_id}/beneficial-owners'
---

The new set must follow these rules:

- Ownership at the top level adds up to at most 100%. The same limit applies to the owners of each business owner.
- Ownership goes at most 3 levels deep.
- There is at most one control person, who is an individual with a `title`.
- Each person has an `email`.
- Only the control person can have 0% ownership.
- The same tax ID can't appear twice.
- The owners of a business owner name it with `parent_ref`.

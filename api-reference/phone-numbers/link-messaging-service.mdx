---
title: 'Link a Phone Number to a Messaging Service'
description: "Ask to link one of your phone numbers to a business (A2P) messaging service."
openapi: 'POST /v1/platform/phone-numbers/{phone_number_id}/messaging-services'
---

The request goes to our team. We register the service with the messaging provider and approve it.

Once it is approved, messages sent from this number go out through the registered service.

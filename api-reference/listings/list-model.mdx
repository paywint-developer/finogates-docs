---
title: 'List Model'
description: "Search your platform's records with filters, sorting and page settings."
openapi: 'POST /v1/platform/listings/{model_key}'
---

Choose the kind of record in the web address with `model_key`, for example `users`, `wallets` or `payment_intents`. Then send your filters, sorting and page settings in the request body.

Results only include your own platform's records. You can never see another platform's data.

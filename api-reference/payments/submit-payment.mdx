This is a `payout` payment, sent by ACH push or digital check auto-deposit. The required `payment_type` chooses the flow:

| Flow | What it does | How to name the bank |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `payment_type=send` | Pays another party. | Use `payee_destination_id` (recommended) or the older `payee_payment_method_id`. |
| `payment_type=transfer` | Withdraws to the user's **own** bank. | Use `payment_method_id`: the user's linked bank, the same one they fund from. |

## Good to know

- If a Plaid-linked bank isn't connected to our payout provider yet, we connect it when needed.
- A virtual bank account can't receive a payout, and we refuse its ID. It only receives deposits, and it can't fund a wallet.

## What happens after you submit

These steps are the same in both flows:

1. The money moves `available → escrow` in the wallet, so it can't be spent.
2. The payment normally starts at `pending_review`. Our team then approves or rejects it. If we have turned on automatic approval for your platform, a payout can skip this step.
3. Once approved, we send the payout. The status becomes `processing`.
4. The bank's result comes later. We send it by webhook, and you can also read it with [Get Payment](/api-reference/payments/get-payment).

| Webhook | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `payment_intent.approved` | The payout was approved. |
| `payment_intent.rejected` | We rejected the payout. The money goes back to the wallet. |
| `payment_intent.executing` | We sent the payout for processing. |
| `payment_intent.succeeded` / `payment_intent.settled` | The payout completed. |
| `payment_intent.failed` | The payout failed, and `failure_reason` says why. The money goes back to the wallet. |

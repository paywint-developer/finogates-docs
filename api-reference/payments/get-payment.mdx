---
title: "Get Payment"
description: "See the full details of one payment."
openapi: "GET /v1/platform/payments/{payment_id}"
---

Use the `payment_id` or the number in `internal_id`. The payment must belong to your platform.

**Documents:** files attached to the payment are listed in `documents`, oldest first. If there are none, you won't see this field. `download_url` only appears on a `clean` document and expires quickly, so get the payment again for a new link.

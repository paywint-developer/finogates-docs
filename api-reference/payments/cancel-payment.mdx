---
title: "Cancel a Payment"
description: "Stop a payment before it reaches the bank."
openapi: "POST /v1/platform/payments/{payment_id}/cancel"
---

You can cancel a payment when it is:

- `pending_review`: waiting for review
- `pending`: approved, but not started
- `processing`, but still waiting with us, such as a same-day payment made after the last cutoff of the day

The money goes back where it came from. The payment ends as `voided` with `failure_reason: cancelled_by_platform`, and we send the `payment_intent.voided` webhook. Cancelling the same payment again just returns it.

## Errors

| Code | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `payment_not_cancellable` (409) | It's too late to cancel. `reason` says why, such as `in_progress` or `already_settled`. Card charges can't be cancelled here. |

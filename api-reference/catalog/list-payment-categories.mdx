---
title: 'List Payment Categories'
description: "Get the payment categories your platform can use."
openapi: 'GET /v1/platform/catalog/payment-categories'
---

Each entry is a number and a name. Send the number with a payment to record what the payment was for. We list only the categories switched on for your platform.

The numbers are stable and never reused, so you can safely store them. They are issued separately in test and live, so check this list in each.

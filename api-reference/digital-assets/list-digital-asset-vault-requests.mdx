---
title: 'List Digital Asset Vault Requests'
description: "Get your platform's digital asset wallet requests, and where each one stands."
openapi: 'GET /v1/platform/digital-assets/vault-requests'
---

Every wallet request needs approval from our team. Each entry shows the decision so far:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `pending_review` | Still being reviewed. |
| `approved` | Our team approved it. |
| `rejected` | Our team rejected it. |

Filter by `status=pending_review` to show only the ones still waiting. Leave it out for the full history.

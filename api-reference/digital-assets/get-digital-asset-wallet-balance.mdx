---
title: 'Get Digital Asset Wallet Balance'
description: "Get the balance of one digital asset wallet, broken down by type."
openapi: 'GET /v1/platform/digital-assets/wallets/{wallet_id}/balance'
---

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `available` | Money spendable now. |
| `pending` | Deposits that haven't fully cleared. |
| `hold` | Money reserved for a withdrawal or transfer in progress. |
| `platform_fee_escrow` | Our own fees. Shown for completeness, and not counted in `total_balance`. |

Any part with no money in it shows zero.

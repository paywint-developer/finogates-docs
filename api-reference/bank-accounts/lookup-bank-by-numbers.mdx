---
title: "Look Up a Canadian or UK Bank by Its Numbers"
description: "Use a bank's numbers to pre-fill your add-bank form."
openapi: 'GET /v1/platform/bank/lookup'
---

For a Canadian bank, you get the institution behind its `institution_number` and a `bank_name` to suggest.

- `country=CA` with `institution_number` (3 digits).
- `country=GB` with `sort_code`. We have no list of UK sort codes yet, so the answer is `found: false`.

## Good to know

- The answer is a suggestion, never a rule.
- `found: false` is not an error.
- Nothing here limits what `POST /v1/platform/bank/add-bank-manually` accepts. A number we do not know is added like any other, with the name and address typed in.
- We check only the format of the number.

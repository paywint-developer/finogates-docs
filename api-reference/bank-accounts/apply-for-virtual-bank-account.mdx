---
title: 'Apply for Virtual Bank Account'
description: "Apply for a virtual bank account for one of your users."
openapi: 'POST /v1/platform/virtual-bank-accounts'
---

A virtual bank account (a "Virtual Account") gives the user their own account number and routing number for receiving money.

## Before this works

We turn the request down unless all of these are true:

- Your platform has the virtual bank account product (`virtual_account`) switched on. You ask for it during the normal sign-up process, and our team must approve that request first.
- Your platform has finished its own identity check (KYC) and business check (KYB), and both are approved.
- The user has passed the identity check (an approved KYC record).
- The user has passed the business check (at least one approved KYB record).
- For a `BUSINESS` account, that KYB's beneficial owners (UBOs) were skipped because the business has none, or they are on file.

A UBO waiting for review does not stop the request. We approve the account once every UBO is approved.

## Hosted verification

When `GET /v1/platform/virtual-bank-accounts/options` returns `hosted_verification: true`:

- A user with approved KYC is enough. Business accounts also need an approved KYB.
- The request must include `industry_type` (from `GET /v1/platform/virtual-bank-accounts/options`) and a `return_url`, or you must have a registered `virtual_account` redirect URL.

After we approve the request, the account moves through these steps:

1. `awaiting_verification`: the user completes a hosted verification page. See `onboarding.action.url`.
2. `verification_in_review`: the user finished the page, and their verification is being checked. You have nothing to do yet. If it fails, the account moves to `verification_failed`. [Get a New Verification Link](/api-reference/bank-accounts/get-virtual-bank-account-verification-link) sends it back to `awaiting_verification`.
3. `awaiting_agreement`: this comes once you call `POST /v1/platform/virtual-bank-accounts/{account_id}/activate`, when `onboarding.stage` is `ready_to_activate`. The user accepts the bank agreement page.
4. `approved`: the account numbers are in `deposit_routes`.

## What happens next

The account starts as `initiated`. Our team reviews every application by hand. We may mark it `applied` once checked, but this step is not always used.

After we approve the request, the account moves through the steps above. Our bank partner then issues the real account and routing numbers, and the account becomes `approved`.

We tell you the result with a webhook, which is an automatic notification to your server:

| Webhook | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `virtual_bank_account.approved` | The application succeeded. |
| `virtual_bank_account.rejected` | The application was declined. |

This webhook is the only sign that the account is ready. **Until the account is `approved`, it must not be used for any payments, payouts or deposits, or linked to a wallet.**

## Avoiding duplicates

- You can include an `idempotency_key`, a unique value you choose. If the same request reaches us twice by mistake, we create the account only once. If you reuse a key with different details, we reject the request.
- The same user can't hold two active accounts of the same type on your platform. We reject a repeat attempt.

## Errors

| Code | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `ubo_not_answered` | The KYB doesn't say whether it has UBOs, they were requested, or none are on file. |
| `ubo_not_approved` | A UBO was rejected. |

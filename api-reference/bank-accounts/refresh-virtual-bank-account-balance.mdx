---
title: 'Refresh Virtual Bank Account Balance'
description: "Get the latest balance of a virtual bank account from our bank partner."
openapi: 'POST /v1/platform/virtual-bank-accounts/{account_id}/refresh-balance'
---

We return the updated account. Our bank partner reports the balance, so a short delay on their side just returns the last known balance. It never returns an error.

This call only means something once the account is approved.

---
title: 'Send an International Payout (Full Payload)'
description: "Send money abroad by sending all the payout details in one call."
openapi: 'POST /v1/platform/international/payouts/global'
---

`POST /v1/platform/international/payouts` uses a recipient you created earlier, by ID. This call takes the recipient and their bank account in the request itself, so you don't have to register them first.

Send:

- The order details: `orderPartnerID`, `orderCurrency`, `orderAmount`, `payoutCurrency` and `payoutBranchID`.
- The recipient: `beneficiary`.
- The recipient's bank: `bankInfo`.
- The wallet to take the money from: `walletId`.

Our team sets up your sender details for your platform, and we apply them automatically. You never send them.

The payout starts on hold for review. We submit it only after one of our operators approves it.

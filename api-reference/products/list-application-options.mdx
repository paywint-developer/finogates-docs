---
title: 'List Application Options'
description: "Get the codes an application may use for an account kind."
openapi: 'GET /v1/platform/products/options'
---

You get the entity types, industries and their sub-industries, purposes of the account, sources of funds, currencies and document types.

This is open to platforms with virtual bank accounts or card transfers. A business card account needs its KYB's `industry` and `sub_industry` from these lists.

Each sub-industry has a suggested four-digit `mcc` to send as the KYB's `business_data.mcc`. We do not apply it for you.

---
title: 'Card Transfers Overview'
description: 'Move money between a user''s card and their wallet.'
---

| Direction | Money moves | Payload types | Cards | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `account_to_card` | Wallet to card | `account` → `card` | Debit | $10.00 |
| `card_to_account` | Card to wallet | `card` → `account` | Debit or credit | $10.00 |

The user needs approved KYC. A business or sole proprietor also needs an approved KYB.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the direction">
    Call [Enable a Card Transfer Direction](/api-reference/card-transfers/enable-card-transfer). Sandbox approves it at once; production waits for Finogates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your redirect URL">
    Call [Set a Redirect URL](/api-reference/redirect-urls/set-redirect-url) with the `card_transfer` purpose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the user's card">
    Call [Open a Card Session](/api-reference/card-transfers/create-card-session) and show the user the returned `url`. For a business or sole proprietor, send `account_kind` and `kyb_id`. Read the `card_id` from [Get a Card Session](/api-reference/card-transfers/get-card-session).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a payment payload">
    Call [Request a Payment Payload](/api-reference/payments/request-payment-payload). Keep the `reference`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the payment">
    Call [Create a Payment from a Request](/api-reference/payments/create-payment-from-request). In master wallet mode, leave out `wallet_id` to use your master wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it">
    The payment starts as `pending_review`, or `pending` if auto-approved, then moves to `processing` and ends `settled` or `failed`. You can [cancel it](/api-reference/payments/cancel-payment) while it is `pending_review` or `pending`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example: account to card

```json POST /v1/platform/payments/request-payload
{
  "source_type": "account",
  "destination_type": "card",
  "amount": "25.00",
  "currency": "USD",
  "idempotency_key": "6f1c2a9e-3d4b-4c8a-9f21-0b7e5d3a1c44"
}
```

```json POST /v1/platform/payments/create-transaction
{
  "reference": "<reference from the payload>",
  "user_id": "<user_id>",
  "card_id": "<card_id>",
  "wallet_id": "<the user's wallet_id>",
  "idempotency_key": "0d9b7e64-1f3a-4b2c-8e5d-7a6c9f0b2e13"
}
```

For card to account, swap the two types.

## Webhooks

- **Card:** `payment_method.card.added`, `.review_approved`, `.review_rejected`
- **Payment:** `payment_intent.created`, `.approved`, `.processing`, `.settled`, `.failed`, `.rejected`, `.voided`

## Sandbox

The card page shows a test form. `4000000000000002` declines transfers, `4000000000000010` is a credit card, and any other number from 12 to 19 digits is a debit card.

## Common errors

Card page errors are listed on [Open a Card Session](/api-reference/card-transfers/create-card-session).

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `product_not_enabled` (403) | The direction isn't enabled. |
| `payload_incomplete` (422) | The card can't take this transfer, or the user's KYC isn't approved. `missing` names it. |
| `not_owned_by_user` (422) | The card or wallet doesn't belong to `user_id`. |
| `payment_not_available` / `amount_below_minimum` (422) | The amount is under the minimum. |
| `card_readd_required` (409) | The user must add the card again. |
| `card_account_pending_verification` (409) | The user's card account isn't ready. Retry later. |
| `payment_cooldown` (429) | The user just made a payment. Retry shortly. |

---
title: 'Card Transfers Overview'
description: 'Move money between a user''s wallet and their own card.'
---

There are two types of card transfer:

| Transfer | What happens | Cards allowed | Minimum amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account to card (`account_to_card`) | Money goes from the user's wallet to their card | Debit only | $10.00 |
| Card to account (`card_to_account`) | Money comes from the user's card into their wallet | Debit or credit | $10.00 |

Before adding a card, the user must pass the identity check (KYC). A business or sole proprietor must also pass the business check (KYB).

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the transfer type">
    Call [Enable a Card Transfer Direction](/api-reference/card-transfers/enable-card-transfer) for each type you want. Sandbox turns it on right away. In production, Finogates approves it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us your website address">
    Call [Set a Redirect URL](/api-reference/redirect-urls/set-redirect-url) with `card_transfer`. The card page only works on this address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the user's card">
    Call [Open a Card Session](/api-reference/card-transfers/create-card-session) and show the user the `url` you get back. For a business or sole proprietor, also send `account_kind` and `kyb_id`. When the user finishes, get the `card_id` from [Get a Card Session](/api-reference/card-transfers/get-card-session).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a quote">
    Call [Request a Payment Payload](/api-reference/payments/request-payment-payload) with the transfer type and amount. Keep the `reference` you get back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the payment">
    Call [Create a Payment from a Request](/api-reference/payments/create-payment-from-request) with the `reference`, user, card and wallet. If master wallet mode is on for your platform, you can leave out `wallet_id` to use your master wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the payment">
    A new payment is `pending_review` while we check it, or `pending` if it was approved automatically. It then moves to `processing` and ends as `settled` or `failed`. You can [cancel it](/api-reference/payments/cancel-payment) before it reaches `processing`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example: account to card

First, get a quote:

```json POST /v1/platform/payments/request-payload
{
  "source_type": "account",
  "destination_type": "card",
  "amount": "25.00",
  "currency": "USD",
  "idempotency_key": "6f1c2a9e-3d4b-4c8a-9f21-0b7e5d3a1c44"
}
```

Then make the payment:

```json POST /v1/platform/payments/create-transaction
{
  "reference": "<reference from the quote>",
  "user_id": "<user_id>",
  "card_id": "<card_id>",
  "wallet_id": "<the user's wallet_id>",
  "idempotency_key": "0d9b7e64-1f3a-4b2c-8e5d-7a6c9f0b2e13"
}
```

`idempotency_key` is any unique value you choose. If the same request reaches us twice, it only counts once.

For card to account, swap the two types: `source_type` is `card` and `destination_type` is `account`.

## Webhooks

We can tell your system when something changes:

- **Cards:** `payment_method.card.added`, `.review_approved`, `.review_rejected`
- **Payments:** `payment_intent.created`, `.approved`, `.processing`, `.settled`, `.failed`, `.rejected`, `.voided`

## Testing in sandbox

The card page shows a test form. Any number from 12 to 19 digits works as a debit card, except these:

| Card number | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `4000000000000002` | Transfers are declined. |
| `4000000000000010` | A credit card. It can add money to a wallet, but can't receive money. |

## Common errors

For errors from the card page, see [Open a Card Session](/api-reference/card-transfers/create-card-session).

| Code | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `product_not_enabled` (403) | This transfer type isn't turned on for your platform. |
| `payload_incomplete` (422) | The card can't be used for this transfer, or the user hasn't passed KYC. `missing` tells you what to fix. |
| `not_owned_by_user` (422) | The card or wallet belongs to someone else. |
| `payment_not_available` / `amount_below_minimum` (422) | The amount is below the minimum. |
| `card_readd_required` (409) | The user needs to add the card again. |
| `card_account_pending_verification` (409) | The user's card account isn't ready yet. Try again later. |
| `payment_cooldown` (429) | The user just made a payment. Wait a moment and try again. |

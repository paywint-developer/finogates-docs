---
title: 'Track a Mailed Document'
description: "See where a mailed document is, and everywhere it has been."
openapi: 'GET /v1/platform/payments/document-mailing/{payment_id}/tracking'
---

You get the current delivery stage and the carrier's full scan history, oldest first.

This call reads what we already have and does not contact the carrier. So it is fine to call it often while a page is open. To force a live read, use [Refresh a Mailed Document's Tracking](/api-reference/document-mailing/refresh-shipped-document-tracking).

## Scan times

Each scan time comes in two forms:

- `occurred_at` is in UTC. Sort by this one.
- `occurred_at_local` is the local time where the parcel was scanned. `utc_offset_minutes` gives the time zone.

Show the local time to people. If you don't, a parcel scanned at 21:14 in Memphis reads as 02:14 the next day.

## Good to know

An empty `events` list is normal for the first several hours after a label is bought. It does not mean tracking has failed.

Check `trackable` to tell this apart from a service that has no barcode at all, such as a letter.
